Have questions?

Here are the most common things people ask.

Read our full FAQs →
Can you write for me?
No — and we get asked more often than you'd think. Writing a dissertation, thesis or assignment for you would mean you learn nothing, and it is a serious form of academic misconduct. The research and the words stay yours; that is what keeps everything we do within academic integrity rules.
Is your service allowed by universities?
Yes. In fact, more than 500 universities refer students to us directly, and some use our content in their own teaching. The one condition, the same one that answers the question about writing for you: the work has to be written by you.
Which universities and degrees do you support?
A wide range, at Bachelor's, Honors, Master's and Doctoral levels, with particular depth in the social sciences. Our expertise is the research process itself rather than one subject area, so the guidance holds whatever you're studying.
Will my work be treated confidentially?
Always. We never share your work and never mention your name anywhere. If your situation calls for it, we're happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement.
What are your prices?
Coaching is sold in hour bundles; editing, transcription and coding are quoted per project, since the right fit depends on your work. Full rates are on our pricing page.
